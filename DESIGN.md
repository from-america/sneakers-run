# Sneakers Run presentation

## Register and direction

Product interface, expressive arcade direction. The audience is a listener
playing a short street escape with a phone, keyboard, or Steam controller.
The artwork carries the city and character; controls explain the next move.

## Type and color

Locally bundled Barlow Condensed Black carries titles with a compact,
human-designed arcade weight; variable Space Grotesk carries
controls, body text, and tabular score numerals. Cream paper, blue-black ink,
yellow action accents, and coral warnings are the four interface color roles.
Color never carries a gameplay instruction alone.

## Composition

A full-bleed illustrated title screen keeps the title and two actions centered
over the city. Active play has a compact separate scoreboard,
a stable street plane, and a dedicated control deck. Menus reclaim the score
and control areas on small screens. Short landscape results use two columns.

## Artwork contract

Keep the approved runner and suit images unchanged. Only existing ChatGPT Image
Gen raster sources enter the packed atlas. Props retain complete silhouettes,
constant proportions, and authored ground pivots. A visible 95-unit passage
fits every roll pose (90 units or less). The player body's forgiving inset is
explicit in game/config.js. The full double jump fits without camera movement.

## Motion and controls

Physics advances at 120 Hz, display interpolation is cosmetic. Backgrounds pan
without mirroring landmarks; the empty street strip scrolls separately. Pausing
freezes actors and physics. Ready/victory animation is cosmetic. Reduced motion
removes particles and invulnerability flicker. Controller and keyboard focus
are visible, and native buttons remain the primary touch interface.

## Deliberate exclusions

No decorative card grid, gameplay-shape SVGs, pulsing collision silhouettes,
remote font requests, or invisible content definitions that imply unimplemented
mechanics. Every shipped encounter uses the same simulation as the tests.
